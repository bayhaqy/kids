Food Chains

Who eats whom in nature.

📚 Lessons

🌻

A food chain shows who eats whom. It starts with a plant — plants make their own food.

🐛

Next comes the herbivore — an animal that eats plants. A caterpillar eats leaves.

🐦

Then a carnivore eats the herbivore. A bird eats the caterpillar.

🦅

A top predator might eat the bird. The chain ends when nothing eats the top one.

♻️

When animals die, decomposers (worms, mushrooms) return nutrients to the soil. The chain starts again!
