The Water Cycle

Earth's water keeps moving — watch it evaporate, form clouds, fall as rain, and flow back to the sea.

Evaporation
Condensation (clouds)
Precipitation (rain)
Collection (rivers/sea)

1. Evaporation

The Sun heats lakes, rivers, and the sea. Water turns into invisible vapor and rises into the sky. Try sliding the "Sun heat" higher — you'll see more vapor rise.

2. Condensation

High up, the air is cooler. The vapor turns back into tiny water droplets — that's a cloud! More vapor means thicker clouds.

3. Precipitation

When clouds get heavy with water, they let it fall as rain, snow, or hail. Press "Make it rain" to see this happen.

4. Collection

Rain falls on the ground. Some soaks in, some flows in rivers back to the sea — and the cycle starts again!
