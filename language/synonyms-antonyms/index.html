Synonyms & Antonyms

Words that mean the same or opposite.

📚 Lessons

🤝

Synonyms are words with the same meaning. Big and large. Happy and glad. Fast and quick.

⚔️

Antonyms are words with opposite meaning. Hot and cold. Up and down. Day and night.

📖

A thesaurus is a book of synonyms. Writers use it to find just the right word.

💭

Synonyms make writing less boring. Instead of "said, said, said", try "whispered, shouted, mumbled".
