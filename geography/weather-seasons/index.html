Weather & Seasons

Sun, rain, wind — and 4 seasons.

📚 Lessons

☀️

WEATHER is what the sky is like today: sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, snowy.

🌸

SPRING: Flowers bloom, baby animals are born, weather gets warmer.

☀️

SUMMER: Hottest season. Long days, perfect for swimming and playing outside.

🍂

AUTUMN (FALL): Leaves change color and fall. Weather cools down.

❄️

WINTER: Coldest season. Snow falls in many places. Wear warm clothes!

🇮🇩

Indonesia has only 2 seasons: wet (rainy) and dry. No snow here!
