The Seven Continents

Meet Earth's 7 giant land masses.

📚 Lessons

🌍

A CONTINENT is a huge piece of land. Earth has 7 continents.

🐘

AFRICA: Sahara desert, lions, elephants, pyramids. Home of the Nile River.

🐼

ASIA: The biggest continent. Home of pandas, tigers, and 4 billion people!

🐻

NORTH AMERICA: USA, Canada, Mexico. Bears, eagles, Grand Canyon.

🦜

SOUTH AMERICA: Amazon rainforest, llamas, dancing, football.

🐨

AUSTRALIA: Smallest continent. Kangaroos, koalas, and the Great Barrier Reef.

🐧

ANTARCTICA: Coldest place on Earth. Only penguins and scientists live there!

🏰

EUROPE: Small but famous. Castles, the Eiffel Tower, and 44 countries.
