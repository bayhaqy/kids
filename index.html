Why kids (and parents) love it

Every lesson is built with kid-friendly design, gradual difficulty, and immediate feedback.

100% Safe

No ads, no sign-ups, no tracking, no chat. Everything runs in your browser — nothing leaves the device.

Bilingual

Toggle between English and Bahasa Indonesia anytime. Same lesson, two languages, with natural text-to-speech in each.

Natural voice

Every lesson can be read aloud in a natural-sounding English or Indonesian voice — perfect for early readers.

Grade-aware

From Kindergarten counting to Grade 9 algebra — pick your level and the content adapts.

Interactive

Drag, draw, type, click — every lesson invites kids to DO, not just read. Simulations make abstract ideas visible.

Self-paced

No timers, no pressure. Kids learn at their own speed, retry as much as they want, and celebrate every win.

For parents & teachers

  • No accounts, no data collection — kids just open the page and learn.
  • Works on any device with a browser — phone, tablet, laptop, Chromebook.
  • Natural text-to-speech in both English and Indonesian — supports early readers and language learners.
  • Aligned with Singapore Primary, Cambridge, IB, IPC, Montessori, and Indonesia's Kurikulum Merdeka.
  • Installable as an app (PWA) — add to home screen for full-screen offline use.
  • Open source — every lesson's code is on GitHub for transparency and contribution.
  • Built by Achmad Bayhaqy — IT leader, parent, and education advocate.
