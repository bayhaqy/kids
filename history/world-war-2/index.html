World War II

What happened and why it matters.

📚 Lessons

🌍

World War II was from 1939 to 1945. Almost every country in the world was involved.

⚔️

Two sides: the Allies (UK, USA, Soviet Union, China, and others) vs the Axis (Germany, Japan, Italy).

🕯️

Millions of people died — soldiers and civilians. It was the deadliest war in history.

💣

The war ended when the USA dropped atomic bombs on Japan in August 1945. Japan surrendered.

🕊️

After the war, the United Nations was created to keep peace. We must remember, so it never happens again.
