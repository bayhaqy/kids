Rhythm & Beats

Clap, stomp, tap — feel the beat.

📚 Lessons

👏

RHYTHM is the pattern of sounds in time. Clap, clap, clap — that's a rhythm!

💓

A BEAT is one steady pulse. Like your heartbeat: thump, thump, thump.

4️⃣

Most songs have 4 beats in a measure. Count: 1-2-3-4, 1-2-3-4.

🎵

Different rhythms make different dances: waltz (3 beats), march (2 beats), pop (4 beats).

🦶

Your body is a drum! Stomp feet, clap hands, snap fingers — make music anywhere.
