Music Notes

Meet Do Re Mi — the music alphabet.

📚 Lessons

🎼

Music is written with NOTES. Each note shows how high or low to sing.

🦌

DO RE MI FA SOL LA TI DO — these are the 7 notes. Like a musical alphabet!

⏱️

Notes also show how LONG to sing. A quarter note = 1 beat. A half note = 2 beats.

🎹

On a piano, the white keys are DO RE MI, and the black keys are sharps and flats.

🎤

When you sing "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star", you are using notes!
