Mixing Colors

Red + yellow = orange. Discover color magic!

📚 Lessons

🔴

RED, YELLOW, BLUE are primary colors. You cannot make them by mixing.

🟠

RED + YELLOW = ORANGE. Mix them and see!

🟢

YELLOW + BLUE = GREEN. Like grass and leaves.

🟣

RED + BLUE = PURPLE. Like grapes and the sky at sunset.

⚫

Mix ALL primary colors together = brown or black. Try it with paint!
