Musical Instruments

Strings, wind, percussion — meet them all.

📚 Lessons

🎸

STRING instruments have strings you pluck or bow. Guitar, violin, piano (it has strings inside!).

🎺

WIND instruments you blow into. Flute, trumpet, clarinet, recorder.

🥁

PERCUSSION instruments you hit or shake. Drum, xylophone, tambourine, maracas.

🇮🇩

Indonesian instruments: Gamelan (percussion), Angklung (bamboo shaker), Suling (bamboo flute).

🎼

A band or orchestra has all kinds of instruments playing together. Beautiful!
