Internet Safety

The internet is amazing — here are 6 rules to stay smart, kind, and safe online.

🤫

1. Keep your personal info private

Never share your full name, home address, phone number, school name, or passwords with strangers online. Even photos can give away where you live.

👋

2. Tell a grown-up if something feels wrong

If a message, picture, or website makes you feel uncomfortable, scared, or confused — tell a parent, teacher, or trusted adult right away. You won't get in trouble.

🤝

3. Be kind online

Words on a screen can hurt just as much as words spoken out loud. Don't say anything online that you wouldn't say to someone's face. If you see bullying, don't join in — tell an adult.

🔒

4. Use strong passwords

A good password is at least 8 characters long and has letters, numbers, and symbols. Don't use your name or birthday. Never share passwords — not even with your best friend.

🤔

5. Not everything online is true

Anyone can post anything on the internet. If a story sounds weird, too good to be true, or scary — check with a trusted adult or a reliable source before believing or sharing it.

⏰

6. Take screen breaks

Staring at screens too long hurts your eyes and body. Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Go outside and play!
