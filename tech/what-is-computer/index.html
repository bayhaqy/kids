What Is a Computer?

Meet the parts: screen, keyboard, mouse.

📚 Lessons

🖥️

A computer is a machine that follows instructions. It helps us learn, play, and create.

📺

The SCREEN shows pictures and words. You are looking at a screen right now!

⌨️

The KEYBOARD has letters and numbers. You press keys to type words.

🖱️

The MOUSE moves a pointer on screen. Click to choose things.

🧠

The CPU is the brain. It thinks very fast — millions of instructions per second!
