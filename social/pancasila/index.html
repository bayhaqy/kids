Pancasila: 5 Principles

Indonesia's foundation of values.

📚 Lessons

⭐

Pancasila has 5 principles. They are the heart of Indonesia.

1️⃣

1st: Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa — Believe in One God. Indonesia has 6 official religions.

2️⃣

2nd: Kemanusiaan yang Adil dan Beradab — A just and civilized humanity. Be kind to all.

3️⃣

3rd: Persatuan Indonesia — Unity of Indonesia. Many islands, one nation.

4️⃣

4th: Kerakyatan ... Bermusyawarah — Democracy through discussion. We talk, we agree.

5️⃣

5th: Keadilan Sosial bagi Seluruh Rakyat Indonesia — Social justice for all.
